About

Welcome to the realm of visual storytelling. I'm a film maker and video editor with 5+ years across production, post-production and design.

I'm driven by a deep passion for bringing stories to life through the art of filmmaking. Whether it's a short film, documentary, promotional video or advertisement, I pour my creativity and technical craft into every frame — on set coordinating the shoot, and in the edit bay shaping the story.

I've cut and coordinated video for Curly Tales, Scoopwhoop, Ok Tested and KK Creates, directed short films with Head Field Films, and run paid campaigns on Google and Meta. My goal is simple: exceed the brief, and leave the audience with something that stays.
